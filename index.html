The Origin  ·  The Founders

The Story

Born From The Garage

It began with a '68 Mustang GT, and a dream rooted in American muscle car culture. What followed is a whiskey with the same precision and appeal as the most iconic cars of an era.

The sight of American muscle on modern streets is a reminder that the right vintage has no equal. Character, heritage, and provenance are a rare combination, yet you will find them all in THROTTLED IN BOND™

In every sip is a memory, and in every bottle is a story. From the barrel selection, to the age, to the expression, THROTTLED IN BOND™ celebrates the vision, passion, and influence of American Muscle.

Watch the Story

THROTTLED IN BOND™ is our expression of nostalgia and passion for American Muscle car culture. We're passionate about hunting for the most exciting barn finds, whether that be a rare muscle car, ready for revival, or a unique whiskey barrel, aged to perfection.

Join us as we celebrate THE SPIRIT OF AMERICAN MUSCLE™.

David & Amy
